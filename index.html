Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Avelluna is a local-first pregnancy companion. Your pregnancy information stays on your phone. We do not run any server that stores your personal data, and we cannot see it.

1Who we are

Avelluna is designed and developed by Afaq Ahmad. Website: honexio.tech. In this policy, “we” and “the app” mean Avelluna.

2Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in a private database and private storage on your phone: your name (optional), due date, journal entries, appointments, photos and captions, weight entries, kick sessions and preferences. We do not receive, copy or upload any of it.

3What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login.
  • No cloud sync, Firebase, Supabase or remote databases.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No transmission of personal data over the internet.

4Internet use

The app works fully offline. If “Public tips” is switched on in settings, it may download a public list of general wellness tips. This request contains no personal information from the app. You can turn it off at any time.

5Permissions

Camera and photo library access are requested only when you choose to add a photo. Selected photos are copied into the app’s private storage and are never uploaded. You can revoke permissions in your phone’s settings at any time.

6Your control

You can edit or delete any entry, photo or record inside the app. “Erase all my data” in settings permanently removes everything, and uninstalling the app also removes its stored data. Because data is only on your device, we cannot recover it for you — consider keeping your own backup of anything important.

7Children’s privacy

Avelluna is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we do not collect personal information from anyone.

8Medical disclaimer

Avelluna provides general information only and is not medical advice. Always consult your doctor or midwife about your health and your baby’s health.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear on this page and in the app.

10Contact

For questions about this policy, please visit honexio.tech.
